# Product name candidates

Checked 2026-07-25 against exact title matches through the Apple public App Store
search for the DK, US, and GB storefronts, with additional web/App Store sanity
searches. The leading candidates had no exact title match in those three
storefronts in that pass. This is not trademark clearance, does not check every
country or pending reservation, and does not reserve a name in App Store Connect.

| Candidate | Why it fits | Main caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DocuRelay** | Broad enough for photos, scans, PDFs, and local file relay; short and brandable. | “Relay” may need a subtitle explaining local SMB transfer. |
| **JournalDrop** | Immediately communicates the destination workflow for the original clinic audience. | Too strongly implies direct journal integration, which the app does not provide. |
| **PraksisCapture** | Distinctive Danish clinic/practice positioning and accurate for photo capture. | Less attractive for international users and document scanning. |
| **JournalBridge** | Clear bridge between device files and an existing journal workflow. | Can overpromise a technical integration with the journal system. |
| **ChartBridge** | More international than JournalBridge and still communicates handoff. | “Chart” is healthcare-specific and may trigger an overly medical expectation. |
| **PaperToChart** | Strongly communicates paper-record digitization. | Too narrow for clinical photos and may imply automatic chart writing. |
| **LocalDoc Bridge** | Makes the local-only architecture explicit. | Less memorable and slightly awkward as a spoken product name. |
| **MedCapture Bridge** | Signals medical capture plus handoff. | Healthcare branding and “medical” claims require extra legal/review care. |
| **ClinicArchive** | Good fit for local gallery and archive behavior. | Understates capture, scanning, PDF, and upload. |
| **ScanToChart** | Clear paper-to-journal use case. | Overpromises direct chart import and excludes clinical photos. |
| **RecordRelay** | Broad, professional, and compatible with several industries. | Generic; needs trademark and broader web clearance. |

## Selected product name

The owner selected **LensBridge** with the subtitle **Private Photo & Scan
Transfer**. The bundle identifier remains unchanged. The App Store Connect app-info
localizations and the in-app user-facing name have been updated accordingly.

On 2026-07-26, the Apple public iTunes Search API returned no exact `LensBridge`
software-title match in the DK, GB, or US responses (exact-title matches: 0 in
each). This is a current availability signal, not trademark clearance or a
guarantee that a pending reservation does not exist; the final name must still
be confirmed in App Store Connect at submission time.

## Previous recommendation

The prior shortlist remains useful as a naming record, but no longer represents
the active recommendation. Keep the bundle identifier unchanged. Before final
submission, check `LensBridge` against trademarks, domains, social handles, and
the intended launch countries.

Names intentionally rejected during this pass: `SecureCapture` (already used as
a named SecureSafe feature), `CareRelay`, and `ClinicFlow` (active products under
those names).
